The Sanctum of Shadows

Hard · Level 5
Kill Clock
Audit

§ Hooks

The Shadow Prelate's ritual must be stopped before it plunges the world into an era of darkness. The oppressive weight of the negative energy is a palpable threat to your very souls. Symbols hint at a cosmic entity being summoned — one beyond even the Prelate's control.

The Scene

§ 1

You stand at the entrance to an ancient, subterranean sanctum. Heavy with dust and the scent of ozone, the chamber's heart holds a dark altar pulsing with malevolent energy. The Shadow Prelate works to complete his forbidden ritual.

Stale air heavy with ozone fills your lungs, mingling with a coppery scent. A low, unsettling hum emanates from the pulsating altar, punctuated by sibilant whispers. Unnatural shadows writhe, and the dim occult glow casts long, distorted figures across the chamber.

Sight Dim light from the altar · crumbling pillars · swirling arcane energy · the hunched figure of the Prelate.
Sound A low, unsettling hum · stone scraping · faint sibilant whispers · the clatter of Sentinel attacks.
Smell Stale dust · ozone from arcane energy · a faint coppery scent like old blood.
Lighting Occult glow from the altar · deep darkness pooling in Hazard zones.

Tactical Zones & Axes

§ 4

The main chamber is approximately 60 ft × 60 ft. The centre is dominated by a dark, pulsating altar — the ritual focus. Around it, patches of unnaturally deep shadow and swirling arcane energy form the Shadow Mire and Ritual Instability zones. Several crumbling pillars and altar structures provide half cover.

● Kinetic Axes

Encounter Axes

Shadow Mire Hazard

Difficult terrain steeped in shadow around the altar.

  • Telegraph: The ground begins to shimmer with unnatural darkness; faint whispers emanate from the earth.
  • Automatic Trigger: When the Sanctum Guardian drops below 50% HP, the Mire becomes actively hostile — imposing Disadvantage on all attacks made from within it.
  • Manual Lever: DC 15 Investigation Study can temporarily disrupt the Mire's magic.

Ritual Instability Flux

The ritual causes surges of wild magic every second round.

  • Telegraph: Visible cracks of raw magic appear across the chamber walls, accompanied by a rising hum.
  • Automatic Trigger: End of every second round (Initiative 0) — a random arcane surge erupts.
  • Manual Lever: DC 16 Arcana Magic — success lets the player choose the surge's effect; failure triggers a random, potentially harmful result.

Design Notes

§ 9
Core philosophy: avoid the "tank-and-spank." The Guardian provides primary damage and control; the Sentinels harass and debuff. The Shadow Mire and Ritual Instability axes add constant pressure and reward players who engage with the battlefield. DPR raised from baseline 120 → 174 to model a fully-rested nova party hitting "brink-of-defeat" — still within the 30–60% lethality window.
